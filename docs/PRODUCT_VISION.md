# Commercial Command Center

Version
v0.1

---

## Vision

Commercial Command Center is the operating system for Lugo Hermanos' commercial organization.

It is not intended to replace the ERP.

It is not intended to be another CRM.

Its purpose is to centralize commercial execution, customer knowledge, project management, and future commercial intelligence into a single application.

---

## North Star

A salesperson should be able to spend an entire workday inside Commercial Command Center without relying on notebooks, spreadsheets, or scattered documents.

---

## Principles

- Execution first.
- ERP remains the source of truth.
- Avoid duplicating ERP data whenever possible.
- Every interaction generates organizational knowledge.
- Customers are the primary business entity.
- Projects represent commercial opportunities.
- Every screen should answer:
  - What should I do next?

---

## Product Pillars

- Execution
- Knowledge
- Commercial Intelligence
- Customer Intelligence
- AI Assistance
